# TGIM Promise Delivery Methodology

The Great Indian Manifesto treats a promise as delivered only when public evidence completes a measurable chain from verified demand to independent citizen verification.

## Five-stage ledger

1. Demand verified by an eligible volunteer or moderator.
2. Promise adopted with a measurable target and deadline.
3. Delivery owner assigned publicly.
4. Work completed with a public evidence URL.
5. Outcome independently verified; delivery owners cannot verify their own work.

## Outcome score

The public score is recalculated from four directly visible factors:

- 40% milestone progress.
- 25% milestone evidence coverage.
- 20% timeliness against milestone due dates.
- 15% aggregated citizen confidence.

An agency's grievance status is never an input to the independent score. A promise receives the `delivered` label only at 90 or above and only when at least one milestone carries an independent `verified` status.

Citizen verdicts are one-per-account and can be updated. `Delivered` contributes fully, `partly delivered` contributes half, `not delivered` contributes zero, and `not sure` is excluded from the decisive denominator. Individual identities are not included in public responses.

## Evidence and audit rules

- Operational milestones can be completed by officers or administrators only with `completed_at` and public evidence.
- The citizen-verification milestone requires `verified` status from a volunteer, moderator, or administrator.
- Every milestone change and citizen verdict writes an immutable audit event.
- Official cases, documents, polling results, and appeals are displayed separately from TGIM's independent state.
- Challenges create a moderator-reviewed dispute and never silently rewrite history.
